## Ground rules applied throughout

1. Every factual statement carries a citation **or an explicit "no source."**
2. Adverse facts receive equal rigor. Nothing omitted for narrative fit.
3. Legal memos inform counsel. They never state what a court "will" do.
4. **Nothing here is sent to any party. All correspondence stays human.**
