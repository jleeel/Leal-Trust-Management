## 0. Scope, method, and limits

- **Mailbox:** jaceleal@gmail.com only. It was read through the Gmail connector using search and read tools only. Nothing was sent, replied to, forwarded, drafted, labeled, archived, trashed, untrashed or otherwise changed. Nothing went to any party.
- **Citation key:** Gmail thread ID, plus the message date and sender. Dates are the sender's local date as shown in the message header or quote line; UTC header timestamps can fall a day later.
- **Read status:** **FULL** means the body of every message was read, either directly or through a complete quoted chain. **PARTIAL** means only some messages were read in full, or only the Gmail snippets (about the first 200 characters) were read. A statement drawn from a PARTIAL thread quotes only the snippet text.
- **Privilege tags:** **[PRIV]** marks client-side (Jace, Steve, Jordan) communications with Ormonde's office (Ormonde, Vieira, Lallas). The same tag is applied, as a presumption, to communications with prior counsel Griswold LaSalle (Johnson, Waite, Askins, Kapheim). This report does not treat privilege as waived. Possible waiver issues are flagged for counsel in §5.
- **Grading:** every finding is marked **[HELPS]**, **[HURTS]** or **[BOTH]** from the plaintiff's point of view. Adverse items get the same treatment as helpful ones.
- **Already read, cross-referenced only:** 1827e17104001f86, 19e2d918e69961b7, 19ed33e5a03baa31 and 19faef5ef5a7be1a, plus the rest of `gmail-extract.md`. Their content is not repeated here.
- **Not covered:**
  - (a) The **second mailbox** (lealdairy@gmail.com / "Leal Dairy") was not searched. Many 2017–2021 settlement messages were addressed to it, and this mailbox holds only the copies sent to jaceleal.
  - (b) **Attachments were not opened.** The connector cannot extract them, so figures that live only in PDF or XLSX files (for example $194,352.65, $327,427.02 and $329,373) could not be checked.
  - (c) Some keyword searches were reviewed only to snippet level on their first page (noted where it happens).
  - (d) Pre-2018 threads with Gin and Childs (2016) and a small set of pre-2022 Griswold LaSalle and Frazer threads were listed but not read (§1b).
- **Credential/PII rule:** no password, full account number or SSN appears in this report. Only last-4 digits are given, marked "credential present — rotate" where a credential is involved.
