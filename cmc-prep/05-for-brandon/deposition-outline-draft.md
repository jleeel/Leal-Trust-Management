### 1A. The buyout is mandatory and was meant to be valued at death

**Purpose:** lock in the contract structure they cannot dispute.

**Foundation:**
- Partnership Agreement Art. XIII.B.1–2 and XIII.J (`post-death-profits-issue.md` §3);
- TIC ¶11 and Settlement Agreement;
- defendants' mediation brief p.4 ("the buyout of the Dairy cannot be completed as the unpaid
  profits need to be paid prior to the sale").

**Questions:**
1. You've read the July 2018 Amended and Restated Partnership Agreement?
2. You understand it says that on Hazel's death "the Partnership **shall** purchase her
   Partnership Interest"?
3. You agree the partnership is required to buy the trust's 37.5%?
4. The price is 90% of appraised value, 10% down, and a 20-year note at 4%?
5. The agreements say the valuation is "as of the date of Hazel's death"?
6. Your side is asking for 37.5% of the profits *after* that date?
7. And interest on those profits from the date of death? (Ex. U: $1,617,761 "includes a
   reasonable rate of interest… from the date of Hazel's passing.")
8. And 90% of the date-of-death value as well?
9. Where in the agreement does it say the trust receives profits after Hazel's death *in
   addition to* the purchase price and 4% interest?

**⚠️ Risk:** Q9 is an open "why/where" question. Use it in deposition only. Their answer will
be §5.1 (the trust is the partner and did not die; *Han v. Hallberg*). Better to hear it now.
