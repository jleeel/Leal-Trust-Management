## 1. Water to the three houses

**Grade: CLIENT-ATTESTED; no document located.** The bank register cannot show it, because
water from the dairy's own wells has no payee. Its cost sits inside the dairy's pumping
electricity, well and pump repairs, and any groundwater fees.

**For us:** an uncompensated, roughly ten-year benefit from partnership resources to Trust
property. It adds to the "benefits flowed to their side" ledger and to the "continued
partner-like treatment" theme (gmail-sweep §2.7).

**Against us:**
- Without metering or a written arrangement, it cannot be quantified. A number made up after
  the fact will be attacked.
- If Steve chose to supply the water while Hazel was alive, the other side will call it a family
  accommodation or gift, not a debt. (Any waiver or estoppel effect is UNVERIFIED and for
  counsel.)
- It is another informal, off-books arrangement, the same pattern A-68 (mobile-home rent) and
  A-69 (cash hoof trimmer) expose.

**To support it (OI-83):**
- addresses and APNs of the three houses and of the well and service lines;
- whether the houses have their own wells or meters;
- a pump-electric meter or usage basis;
- when it began and who asked;
- any email about it.

**For us:**

  expensed items would *raise* the estate's draws and lower its capital, which favors the buyout

- The trustees knew. On 3/12/2025 Ashley asked and Jace confirmed that the partnership paid the

  through the business" (Gmail `19aeb39a4de4ac29`).

  (STEVE.HAZEL PERSONAL EXPENSES.xlsx, chronology 2015-01-01). Steve's family appeared on it too.
  That is transparency, not concealment.

**🔴 Against us (A-75):**
1. **Bookkeeping reliability.** Our books, kept by Jace, expensed a non-partner's personal car
   insurance as partnership insurance for ten years. They also reimbursed "Danielle Ins" to Jace
   as "Supplies," and charged a Danielle fee to Steve's donations. Defendants' CPAs (Edwards &
   Barber) will use that to attack every classification in 505000 and the parity analysis,
   whichever way the dollars cut.
2. **Tax.** Personal expenses deducted as business expenses on the partnership's returns (2016
   onward) are a tax-exposure question for Frazer, not for us. Raise it with Frazer, privately,
   through counsel.
3. **The same pattern on our side.** The 2017 schedule listed partnership-paid personal expenses
   for Steve ($49,122.05) and Jace/Jordan ($21,304.82) as well as Hazel ($66,072.53). The other
   side can say everyone's personal items went through the dairy. That neutralizes the "their
   side took more" point unless it is measured carefully.
4. **Authority after death.** After 9/23/2022 the partnership kept paying Danielle's insurance
   (64 payments, $26,325.31, calc). Who asked for it, and on whose account? If no trustee
   instruction exists, charging it to the estate's capital can be challenged. If the trustees
   asked for it, that helps us.
