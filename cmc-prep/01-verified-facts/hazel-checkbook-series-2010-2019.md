## 3. Grade and significance (both ways)

**For us:**
- The register shows a decade of Hazel's own checks paying her household costs from the
  partnership account. **About $98,775 is plainly personal by payee** and was *booked as dairy
  expenses*. Up to $196,724 in total was coded to partnership expense accounts.
- Every such item **understates Manuel's and Hazel's withdrawals.** Steve's side, at 50% before
  2015 and 55% after, bore part of each one.
- That rebuts "Steve took more" (A-1, A-60) at the level of what the books *don't* show. The
  Pismo carrying costs, on a non-partnership house half-owned by Susan from 10/2016, are the
  cleanest example.

**Against us, or limiting:**
1. **Our side kept the books.** Jace coded these checks. This is the same reliability exposure
   as A-75, and it shows the coding problem predates the litigation.
2. **Most of it predates the Settlement**: $186,446 before 12/7/2017.
   - The parties agreed a capital schedule as of 12/31/2016. Frazer, 2/22/2018: "Adjust capital
     on the financial statement to match the agreed to 75/25 schedule."
   - Whether pre-2017 items can be reopened is for counsel (UNVERIFIED). It may be usable only
     as rebuttal.
3. **Some 2015–2017 items were already disclosed** in the 12/6/2017 personal-expense schedule
   and "Draw Reconciliation" (Hazel $66,072.53).
4. **Not all of it is Hazel's.**
   - The gardener also served Steve: Johnson, 12/6/2017, "the gardner for your dad, Hazel and
     church… Juan Iglesias."
   - AT&T, PG&E, Ford Credit and Tulare County taxes may be dairy costs.
   - The 2017 schedule shows Steve's family had personal items expensed too: Steve $49,122.05;
     Jace and Jordan $21,304.82.
5. **Authorship beyond the three scans is CLIENT-ATTESTED.** Hazel was 85–87 by 2017–2019, and
   Susan was her POA by 2021. Signature images are needed for any check relied on.
