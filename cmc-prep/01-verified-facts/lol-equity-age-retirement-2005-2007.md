## Grade and significance (both ways)

**The payments are SUPPORTED** by the checks and letters (copies; negotiation and deposit not
shown). Three things are **INDETERMINATE:**
1. **Whose equity it was.** Land O'Lakes patronage equity normally accrues on the milk a member
   ships.
   - If member no. 2017201 was the account the dairy shipped under after 1983, much of this
     equity was earned by **partnership** milk. It was paid out to Manuel and Hazel
     personally, and to Hazel at a time she was **not a partner**.
   - If it was Manuel's pre-1983 sole-proprietorship equity, or the membership was his
     personally, it was theirs.
   - No. 2041468 is a second membership, also unexplained.
2. **Whether the books recorded it.** The reviewed statements (S-4) show large "Personal"
   withdrawals for Manuel in exactly these years:
   - 2005: $243,256, against Steve's $10,124;
   - 2007: $220,838, against Steve's $74,360.
   That is *consistent with* the checks having been run through the partnership as Manuel's
   withdrawals. It is not proof. If they were recorded, they are already in the parity numbers
   and favor our side. If they were not, they are unrecorded distributions of a partnership
   asset.
3. **How the client came to hold it.** The scan's metadata (eCopy ShareScan) suggests a law-firm
   copier. It may be an enclosure to the 2016 Gin letters. Ask the client.

**What helps:** about $286,000 of cooperative equity went to Manuel and Hazel personally in
2005–2007, including **$137,197 to Hazel individually**. If it was partnership equity, it bears
directly on the draw-parity comparison and on any "Steve took more" narrative (A-1, A-60).

**What cuts against it:**
- Age-retirement redemptions are paid to the member of record. LOL's records name Manuel and
  Hazel as the owners, so the other side will say the equity was theirs.
- This is 2005–2007, before the 2017 Settlement. Whether accounting claims for that period
  survive is a question for counsel (UNVERIFIED; see the Settlement ¶13/¶15 release scope).
- It cannot be used without the partnership's LOL membership and equity statements.
