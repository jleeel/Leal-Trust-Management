PRIOR PERIOD (1983–2015). Partnership formed March 10, 1983 (Manuel/Steve; Steve’s $212,035 note; Manuel’s signature recited as representing the community property interest of Manuel and Hazel). Reviewed financial statements exist for FY1993 and FY1998–FY2025; FY1994–FY1996 are not located in available records. On Manuel’s death the federal K-1 split became Steve 55 / Hazel 37.5 / Bypass Trust 7.5 and held through 2021.
  1. Mar 25, 2015
    Manuel C. Leal dies. Under his Living Trust: 30% partnership interest to Steve; 7.5% to the Bypass Trust; Hazel at 37.5%. Hazel and Steve become successor co-trustees.
  2. 2016–2017
    First trust-administration dispute — Esraelian demand letters; our side’s voluntary production of ledgers and registers within days; Hazel’s own mediation brief (Oct 6, 2017) asserting a ~$4.4M note claim against Steve.
  3. Nov 10, 2017
    During settlement negotiations, Hazel restates her trust and disinherits Steve’s line (Third Amended and Complete Restatement).
  4. Dec 7, 2017
    Settlement Agreement and Mutual Release executed. ¶7: buyout at 90% of appraised value, 10% down, 20-yr amortization, 4%. ¶5: Morgan Stanley account divided. ¶6: $350,000 note payment within 6 months. ¶8: monthly accountings.
    ¶5 division and ¶6 payment: no performance located in the record. See ledger.
  5. Jun–Jul 2018
    Executed Tenants-in-Common Agreement (6/1) and Amended & Restated Partnership Agreement (7/24): Hazel’s death is a defined “Dissociating Event”; buyout is mandatory at 90% of appraised value with discounts; 4% note is the delay compensation.
  6. Sept 22/23, 2022
    Hazel J. Leal dies. THE VALUATION DATE. Defendants become Successor Co-Trustees. date variant 9/22 vs 9/23 — certificate outstanding
  7. Oct 31, 2022
    Five weeks after death, our counsel initiates: representation, closing Manuel’s Trust, and exercise of the purchase option.
  8. Feb–Mar 2023
    Appraisers agreed: Reddington (partnership, Feb 3–6); Correia-Xavier — proposed by defendants’ counsel (Feb 15), accepted by ours (Mar 9). Joint scope call Apr 6; Xavier still awaiting scope May 8.
  9. Aug 8, 2023
    Correia-Xavier appraisal issued — $14,220,000 (date-of-death value, 100% ownership, no discounts), transmitted to defendants’ counsel. Aug 18: her written promise — “I will send it over to Brandon by next week.”
  10. Nov–Dec 2023
    Defendants reject the appraiser they proposed (Nov 28) after retaining J. Hower & Associates over written objection; Hower issues $18,100,000 (Dec 13). Neither report is delivered.
  11. Jul 10, 2024
    Reddington having died, defendants unilaterally retain Moss Adams over same-day objection (then assert “Jace agreed”).
  12. Sep–Nov 2024
    Moss Adams review: both real-property appraisals credible, no errors (Sep 10). Moss Adams partnership valuation $2,840,000 (Nov 18, discounts applied). Sep 26: Ashley acknowledges holding both appraisals; proposes averaging.
  13. Jan 17, 2025
    Appraisals finally delivered — 17.3 months after Correia-Xavier — emailed to the client, not counsel: “forward them to Brandon Ormonde, your attorney, at your discretion.” ≈$272,000 of 4% interest accrued in the interval.
  14. Mar 3, 2025
    Plaintiff’s offer — $3,950,565.28, with full computation — six weeks after receiving the appraisals.
  15. June 19, 2025
    Defendants’ “Final” counteroffer — $7,359,766 (15% discount) + $1,617,761 claimed unpaid profits — after 3½ months and two status letters.
  16. Oct 16, 2025
    COMPLAINT FILED — ten causes; specific performance of the buyout at the core. Defendants have not responded (open-ended extension); no discovery served by either side.
  17. Jan 28, 2026
    Mediation (Hon. Timothy Volkmann (Ret.), JAMS) — no resolution; defendants’ demand ~$6,000,000.
  18. May 18, 2026

  19. Jul 2–9, 2026
