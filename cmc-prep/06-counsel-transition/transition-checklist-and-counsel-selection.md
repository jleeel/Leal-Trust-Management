3. **Interview candidates now** (Section C). Conflicts check FIRST, using the names list below.
4. **Engage the new attorney, then file the Substitution of Attorney** (Judicial Council form;
   client + outgoing + incoming sign; serve and file). Deadlines do NOT pause: the Sept 15
   package date and Oct 13 CMC stand regardless.
5. **Timing options** (decide with the new attorney, not before):
   - *Substitute promptly:* new counsel owns the CMC statement, the amendment, the discovery
     launch, and any fresh § 998. Cleanest if engaged by early–mid September.
   - *Substitute after the CMC:* avoids a scramble but lets prior counsel set the schedule and
     positions at the CMC that the new attorney inherits. Generally the worse option if the goal
     is speed.
6. **Preserve everything.** All files, emails, drafts — including prior counsel's work. The new
   attorney may need the record of the § 998 service facts for the substantial-compliance
   argument, and preservation is simply required. Do not editorialize about prior counsel in
   writing anywhere.
7. **Do not sign anything with the other side, and send nothing to them, during the gap.**
